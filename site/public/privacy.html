Privacy Policy

Hồi Sau · Last updated: 5 November 2026 · Tiếng Việt

This policy explains what information Hồi Sau collects, why, how long it is kept, who it is shared with, and how you can have it deleted. It covers the Hồi Sau comic channels on Facebook, Instagram, Threads and TikTok, the website https://hoisau.dangtrantai.com/, and the Hồi Sau publishing tool (the "Service").

1. Who we are

Hồi Sau is an AI-drawn office comedy comic run by Đặng Trần Tài, an individual based in Hà Nội, Việt Nam ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the information described here. Contact details are in section 11.

2. Information we collect

2.1 From the Hồi Sau channel owner's TikTok account

The publishing tool connects only to TikTok accounts that belong to Hồi Sau. When the channel owner logs in with TikTok ("Login Kit") and grants permission, we receive:

  • Basic profile (scope user.info.basic): the account's open ID and display name, used to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used to upload content.
  • Upload permission (scope video.upload): lets the tool send videos and photo posts made by Hồi Sau to the account's TikTok inbox as drafts. The owner reviews, edits and publishes each draft in the TikTok app.
  • Post status returned by TikTok (for example "sent to inbox" or "published") and the post ID, used to avoid sending the same post twice.

We do not access the TikTok accounts, profiles, videos, followers, messages or any other data of TikTok viewers or of any other TikTok user. We do not read the connected account's messages, followers, likes or analytics.

2.2 From people who interact with Hồi Sau on Meta platforms

  • Public comments and messages that you send to the Hồi Sau Facebook Page, Instagram or Threads account, with the display name and platform ID that come with them.

2.3 From website visitors

The website is a static site hosted on Firebase Hosting (Google). It sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operation.

3. How we use information

  • To upload Hồi Sau's own comic videos and photo posts to Hồi Sau's TikTok account as drafts, at the owner's request.
  • To show the connected account name and connection status in the publishing tool.
  • To pick ideas for the next episode from audience comments, and to credit the commenter by display name in that episode.
  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To keep the Service secure and to prevent duplicate or unwanted posts.

We do not use any information for advertising, profiling, or selling, and we do not use TikTok data to train AI models.

4. AI processing

To rate story ideas, the text of public comments on Meta platforms may be sent to Meta's AI models (Llama API). Only the comment text is sent, for this purpose only. TikTok data is never sent to any AI model.

5. Where information is stored

  • TikTok tokens, open ID and display name are stored only in a configuration file on the channel owner's own computer. They are not stored on our website or on any shared server.
  • Comments and the record of published posts are stored on the same computer and in our own private files.
  • Images and videos that we publish are stored on the website so that TikTok and Meta can fetch them.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties, except:

  • With the platform itself when we post to it (TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, Threads), under that platform's own terms and privacy policy.
  • With Meta's Llama API to rate comment ideas, as described in section 4.
  • With Google Firebase Hosting, which serves the website.
  • When required by law.

7. How long we keep information

  • TikTok access tokens expire after 24 hours and refresh tokens after 365 days. All TikTok tokens and profile data are deleted from the owner's computer when the account is disconnected, or at the latest when the refresh token expires.
  • The record of published posts (post ID and status) is kept for up to 12 months.
  • Comments, messages and display names are kept for up to 12 months, or until you ask us to delete them.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Revoke TikTok access: in the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions, and remove "Hồi Sau". The tool can then no longer upload to that account.
  • Delete your data: follow the data deletion instructions or contact us. We delete your comments, messages and name from our records within 7 days and confirm when done.
  • You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or object to its use. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as Việt Nam's Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP on personal data protection or the EU GDPR.

9. Security

Tokens are kept only on the owner's computer, are never shown in logs or published, and are sent only to TikTok over HTTPS. Every post is reviewed by the owner before it is published. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

10. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Dùng để làm gì

Gửi truyện của Hồi Sau vào nháp TikTok, chọn ý cho tập sau, ghi tên người góp ý, và trả lời tin nhắn. Không bán dữ liệu, không quảng cáo. Dữ liệu TikTok không dùng để huấn luyện AI. Nội dung bình luận trên Meta có thể được gửi tới mô hình Llama của Meta để chấm điểm ý tưởng.

Lưu ở đâu, bao lâu
